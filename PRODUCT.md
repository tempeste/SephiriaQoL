# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sephiria players who want lightweight run information and multiplayer coordination
without leaving the game. Panels are used during active play, often at high display
resolutions, so information must remain quickly readable and get out of the way.

## Product Purpose

Sephiria QoL adds independently maintained conveniences while preserving the
game's authority and normal behavior. Success means each feature feels native,
communicates its state immediately, and remains optional for players who do not
need it.

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, handcrafted, and wood-built. The interface should feel like a
compact fantasy notice board or carved adventurer's kit beside Sephiria's dark
pixel-RPG menus rather than resembling a futuristic dashboard or polished app.

## Anti-references

- Thin cyan instrumentation and sci-fi dashboard styling
- Glassy, glowing, or highly rounded panels
- Flat, perfectly clean application chrome with no material character
- Decoration that competes with combat information
- Color-only status communication

## Design Principles

- Belong in the game: echo Sephiria's visual character without packaging its assets.
- Preserve the glance: names, values, and actions remain readable during play.
- Show state twice: pair color with text, numbers, shape, or position.
- Share one vocabulary: every overlay uses the same frames, controls, and hierarchy.
- Stay unobtrusive: compact panels, predictable controls, and no decorative motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain high text contrast, preserve scalable interfaces for Retina displays, and
never rely on hue alone for player identity, status, or data comparison.
